This record presents a conceptual essay intended as a supplementary interpretive text to the academic framework of the Negentropy-Oriented Society.

It does not introduce new empirical findings or formal models. Instead, it offers a cultural and societal thought experiment—Japan as a recoverable society—to explore how recoverability-centered social design may manifest in a real-world context.

This essay is provided to support conceptual clarity, cross-disciplinary dialogue, and broader interpretation of the associated research papers within the Negentropy-Oriented Society community.
